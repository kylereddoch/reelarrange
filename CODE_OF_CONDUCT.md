# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ReelArrange a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on behavior, code, and outcomes rather than people.
- Accept correction and make a good-faith effort to repair harm.
- Protect private information found in logs, screenshots, and media paths.
- Avoid sexualized language, personal attacks, harassment, trolling, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code. Temporary or permanent participation restrictions may be applied when necessary.

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the repository owner privately through the GitHub profile or another private contact method published by the owner. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
